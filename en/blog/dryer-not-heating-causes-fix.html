Dryer Not Heating: Causes and What to Check

Quick Answer

The usual causes are a blocked lint filter or vent, a failed heating element, a faulty thermostat or thermal fuse, or a power problem. Clean the filter and vent first.

By the Ahmad Automatic Washing Repair team · Updated October 2026

A dryer that spins but stays cold wastes time and electricity. This guide lists the common causes of a dryer not heating, what you can check safely, and what needs a technician.

A blocked vent is the cheapest cause to rule out, so clean the filter and check the vent hose before anything else. A dryer that runs but stays cool, or shuts off early, usually points to a heating element, a thermostat or a thermal fuse.

Some dryers need a dedicated power supply. If the drum turns but nothing heats, check the breaker too. Parts that touch the heating circuit should be tested by a technician, because mains power is involved.

Common Causes

  • Blocked lint filter or vent
  • Failed heating element
  • Faulty thermostat or thermal fuse
  • Tripped breaker or weak power supply
  • Overloaded drum

What to Check First

  1. Empty and rinse the lint filter
  2. Check the vent hose for kinks and lint
  3. Check the breaker and the plug
  4. Try a smaller load. If it still does not heat, call a technician

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does my dryer spin but not heat?

Usually a blocked vent or a failed heating element, thermostat or thermal fuse. A technician can test each part.

Is it safe to open a dryer to check the heating element?

No. Switch it off and call a technician, because the parts carry mains power.
