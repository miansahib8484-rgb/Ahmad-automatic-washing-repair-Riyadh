Dryer Not Heating: Causes and What to Check

Quick Answer

A dryer that runs but does not heat usually has a blocked vent or lint filter, a failed heating element, or a tripped thermal fuse or thermostat. Check airflow first.

By the Ahmad Automatic Washing Repair team · Updated October 2026

The drum turns, the machine hums, and the clothes come out cold and damp. A dryer that does not heat is very annoying, especially when the laundry basket is full.

It is also one of the more diagnosable faults. Start with airflow, because it is the cheapest thing to fix and the most common cause.

What to Check First

  1. Clean the lint filter, and wipe lint from the filter housing.
  2. Check the vent hose and outlet for blockages, kinks or crushing.
  3. Make sure the dryer is plugged in securely and the socket works. Check your breaker panel.
  4. Run a short, small load. A drum overloaded with heavy towels may seem as though it is not heating.
  5. If the drum is still cold after ten minutes, stop and book a visit.

If Airflow Is Fine

The heating element

It can burn out over time. Without it, the dryer turns but never heats.

Thermostat or thermal fuse

These cut the heat to prevent overheating. A blocked vent can trip them, and once tripped they need replacing.

Control board or sensors

Less common, but possible, especially if the machine also behaves oddly in other ways.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my dryer not heating?

Common causes are a blocked vent, a failed heating element or a tripped thermostat. A technician can check each one.

Why is my dryer running but clothes stay wet?

Usually restricted airflow. Clean the lint filter and check the vent.

Can I replace a heating element myself?

It is best left to a technician, as it involves live electrical parts.
