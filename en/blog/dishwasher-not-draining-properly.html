Why Is My Dishwasher Not Draining Properly?

Quick Answer

A dishwasher that will not drain usually has a clogged filter, a kinked drain hose or a blockage where it joins the sink pipe. If those are clear, the drain pump may have failed.

By the Ahmad Automatic Washing Repair team · Updated October 2026

You open the dishwasher and find a shallow pool of grey, slightly sad water at the bottom. It is one of the most common dishwasher complaints, and one of the more fixable.

Here is the order we would work through it.

Check in This Order

  1. Switch the dishwasher off at the wall. Scoop out standing water with a cup and a cloth.
  2. Remove the bottom rack and take out the filter. Rinse off food and grease, and put it back firmly.
  3. Look at the drain hose behind or under the machine for kinks or squashing.
  4. If the dishwasher drains into the sink pipe, run the sink tap. If the sink is slow or blocked, the problem may be there and not in the dishwasher.
  5. Run a short cycle and listen. A humming pump that moves no water suggests a jam. A silent pump suggests a pump or board fault.

Why It Keeps Happening

Most dishwashers drain badly because they are asked to move food waste without much help. Scraping plates before loading, cleaning the filter every few weeks and running hot water at the tap before a cycle all help. If your water is hard, white scale can also gum up the works, as we explain in our guide on limescale.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my dishwasher not draining?

Usually a clogged filter, a kinked hose or a blocked sink connection. If those are clear, the pump may have failed.

Is it normal to have a little water in the bottom?

A small amount in the sump is normal on some models. A visible pool is not.

Can I fix a dishwasher drain pump myself?

It is better left to a technician. Checking the filter and the hose is safe, as long as the machine is switched off.
