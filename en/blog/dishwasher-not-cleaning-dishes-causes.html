Dishwasher Not Cleaning Dishes Properly: Causes and Fixes

Quick Answer

A dishwasher that leaves dishes dirty or cloudy usually has a clogged filter, blocked spray arms, hard water, the wrong amount of detergent or a loading problem. Check these before calling.

By the Ahmad Automatic Washing Repair team · Updated October 2026

Gritty plates, cloudy glasses and a film that was not there last month. The first thought is that the dishwasher is dying. It rarely is. A dishwasher is mostly a pump and a spinning arm, and they rely on a clean path for the water.

Here is what to look at, from the free fixes to the ones that need a technician.

Causes, in the Order We Check

The filter

Food and grease collect there and reduce spray pressure. Clean it every few weeks.

The spray arms

Check that the holes are clear and the arms spin freely. A toothpick works for blocked holes.

Loading

Tall items blocking the arm, or plates stacked so water cannot reach them, make a surprising difference.

Detergent

Too little leaves food behind. Too much leaves film. Use dishwasher detergent only, never hand soap.

Hard water

White cloudiness on glasses often means hard water. A rinse aid helps. See limescale.

Water temperature or heating

If the water never gets hot, the heating element or a sensor may be at fault.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my dishwasher not cleaning properly?

Common causes are a clogged filter, blocked spray arms, hard water, the wrong detergent amount or poor loading.

Why are my glasses cloudy?

Usually hard water or too much detergent. A rinse aid often helps.

Can I use regular dish soap in a dishwasher?

No. It foams excessively and can overflow. Use dishwasher detergent only.
