Dishwasher Not Cleaning Dishes Properly: Causes and Fixes

Quick Answer

Poor cleaning is usually a clogged filter or spray arm, overloaded racks, hard-water scale, the wrong detergent, or a weak water supply or pump.

By the Ahmad Automatic Washing Repair team · Updated October 2026

When a dishwasher stops cleaning, the cause is often simple. This guide goes from the easy checks to the faults that need a technician, so you can fix what you can and book help for the rest.

Start with the basics: scrape plates, do not overload the racks, and make sure the spray arms spin freely. Clean the filter at the bottom of the tub, because a clogged filter sends dirty water back onto the dishes.

If dishes still come out gritty, cloudy or with a film, the cause may be scale, too little detergent or rinse aid, or low water pressure. A weak circulation pump or a faulty inlet valve needs a technician.

Common Causes

  • Clogged filter
  • Blocked spray arm holes
  • Overloaded or badly loaded racks
  • Scale, wrong detergent or no rinse aid
  • Weak circulation pump or low water supply

What to Check First

  1. Clean the filter and the spray arm holes
  2. Reload the racks so water reaches every item
  3. Use the right detergent and rinse aid
  4. Run a hot empty cycle with a dishwasher cleaner

Frequently Asked Questions

Why are my dishes cloudy after the dishwasher runs?

Hard-water scale, too much detergent or no rinse aid are common causes. Clean the filter and try a dishwasher cleaner.

When should I call a technician for a dishwasher that does not clean?

If the filter, spray arms and loading are fine and the dishes are still dirty, the pump or inlet valve may need testing.
