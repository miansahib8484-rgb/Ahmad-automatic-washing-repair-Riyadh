How to Clean a Washing Machine: A Simple Monthly Routine

Quick Answer

Wipe the door seal, clean the detergent drawer and pump filter, run a hot maintenance wash with a washing machine cleaner once a month, and leave the door ajar after use.

By the Ahmad Automatic Washing Repair team · Updated October 2026

It feels odd to clean a machine whose whole job is cleaning. But a washer is a warm, damp, dark box full of detergent residue, which is exactly what mould and bad smells like. A little routine keeps it fresh and avoids some repairs.

This takes about fifteen minutes a month.

A Simple Monthly Routine

  1. Wipe the door seal with a damp cloth, including the folds where lint and hair hide.
  2. Take out the detergent drawer, rinse it and wipe the cavity behind it.
  3. Clean the pump filter (unplugged, with a towel and tray ready). See our drain guide.
  4. Run an empty hot cycle with a washing machine cleaner, following the product and the manual.
  5. Wipe the drum and the glass, then leave the door and drawer ajar so everything dries.

Small Habits That Matter

Use the amount of detergent on the pack. More is not cleaner, and the extra builds up as residue. Take wet clothes out soon after the cycle, because a damp load left in the drum is a smell waiting to happen. And if your water leaves white crust on taps and kettles, read our note on limescale in washing machines.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I clean my washing machine?

A quick wipe of the seal and drawer weekly, and a maintenance wash and filter clean monthly, is a good habit.

Why does my front load washer smell musty?

Damp is usually trapped in the door seal and detergent drawer. Wipe them and leave the door ajar between washes.

Can I use vinegar to clean my machine?

Check your manual first. Some makers advise against acids on rubber seals. A washing machine cleaner made for the job is the safer choice.
