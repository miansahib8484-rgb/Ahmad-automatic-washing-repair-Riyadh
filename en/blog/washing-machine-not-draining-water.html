Washing Machine Not Draining Water: Causes and Fixes

Quick Answer

A washing machine that will not drain is usually a clogged pump filter, a kinked or blocked hose, or a pump that has failed. The filter is the one you can check yourself.

By the Ahmad Automatic Washing Repair team · Updated October 2026

You open the door and the clothes are floating. It is one of the more annoying washing machine moments, but it is also one of the more fixable ones. Most of the time the water is not gone for a simple reason: something is in the way.

Here is what to check, in the order we would check it, and when it is time to call someone.

Why It Happens

  • A clogged pump filter, usually lint, a sock, a coin or a hair tie
  • A drain hose that is kinked behind the machine
  • A blocked drain pipe at the wall
  • A drain pump that has worn out or jammed
  • A water level sensor or control board fault

What to Check, Step by Step

  1. Unplug the machine. Put a towel and a shallow tray down, because water will come out.
  2. Open the small pump filter panel, usually at the bottom front on a front loader. Unscrew the filter slowly and let the water drain into the tray.
  3. Take out whatever you find. It is often lint and one unlucky sock. Rinse the filter and put it back firmly, because a loose cap leaks.
  4. Pull the machine forward a little and check that the drain hose is not folded or squashed. Also check the pipe at the wall is not blocked.
  5. Plug in and run a short spin or drain cycle. If the water still stays, the pump itself is the next suspect.

A Few Honest Warnings

If you have just run a hot wash, the water in the filter will be hot, so wait. If the machine hums but does not pump, that points to a jammed pump. If it is completely silent, it can be the pump, the board or a sensor. In both cases a technician can tell quickly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my washing machine not draining water?

The usual causes are a clogged pump filter, a blocked or kinked hose or a failed pump. A technician can check each one.

Is it safe to open the drain filter?

Yes, if the machine is unplugged and you have a towel and tray ready. Some water will come out, and it can be hot after a hot wash.

What does OE mean on an LG?

OE is a drain error. The checks are the same: hose, then pump filter.
