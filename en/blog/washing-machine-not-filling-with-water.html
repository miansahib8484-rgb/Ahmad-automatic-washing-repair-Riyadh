Washing Machine Not Filling With Water: Causes and Fixes

Quick Answer

A washing machine that will not fill with water is usually caused by a closed tap, a kinked inlet hose or a blocked inlet filter. If those are clear, the inlet valve may have failed.

By the Ahmad Automatic Washing Repair team · Updated October 2026

When the drum stays dry, or fills so slowly that the cycle stalls, the problem usually sits on the water supply side. Here is what to check on the machine, and when to book a washing machine repair visit in Riyadh.

Common Causes

  • Tap closed or only partly open
  • Inlet hose kinked or squashed behind the machine
  • Blocked inlet filter at the back of the machine
  • Faulty water inlet valve
  • Door not detected as closed, so the cycle never starts filling
  • Water level sensor or control board fault

What to Check First

  1. Make sure the water tap is fully open and that other taps in the home are running normally
  2. Unplug the machine, pull it forward a little and check that the inlet hose is not kinked
  3. Close the tap, unscrew the hose from the machine and rinse the small mesh filter under running water
  4. Reconnect the hose, open the tap, close the door firmly and run a short cycle
  5. Still no water? Book a repair, as the inlet valve or a sensor may need replacing

When It Is Not a Simple Fix

If the tap, hose and filter are all clear and the machine still does not fill, the inlet valve is the next suspect. It opens and closes electrically, so it can fail even when the water supply is fine.

A door lock fault or a water level sensor fault can also stop the fill. These need a technician, so unplug the machine and avoid opening any panels yourself.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my washing machine not filling with water?

The usual causes are a closed tap, a kinked hose, a blocked inlet filter or a faulty inlet valve. A technician can check each one.

Is it safe to clean the inlet filter myself?

Yes, if the machine is unplugged and the tap is closed. Keep a towel ready because a little water will come out of the hose.

What if the machine fills very slowly?

Slow filling often points to low water pressure or a partly blocked inlet filter. Clean the filter first, then call us if it stays slow.
