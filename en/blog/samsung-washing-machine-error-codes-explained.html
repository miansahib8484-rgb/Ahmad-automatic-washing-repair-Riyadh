Samsung Washing Machine Error Codes: What 4E, 5E, dE and UE Mean

Quick Answer

On Samsung washers, 4E or 4C means a water supply problem, 5E or 5C a drain problem, dE or dC a door error and UE or UC an unbalanced load.

By the Ahmad Automatic Washing Repair team · Updated October 2026

Samsung codes are short and, once you know them, quite specific. Four of them cover most of what people ask us about. Here is what each means and what to check first.

Samsung Codes and What to Try

CodeWhat it usually meansWhat to try first
4E / 4CWater supply problemOpen the tap fully, check the hose, clean the inlet filter
5E / 5CWater is not drainingCheck the drain hose and clean the pump filter
dE / dCDoor not detected as closedClose firmly, clear the seal, try again
UE / UCUnbalanced loadSpread the clothes out and restart

Other codes you may see on some models include 1E (a water level sensor problem) and 3E (a motor sensor problem). They usually need a technician.

Three Things Most People Overlook

First, a 4E can be as simple as a tap that was turned off after a plumber visited, so check that before anything else. Second, a 5E that returns every few cycles is a warning that the pump is struggling. Deal with it before it fails completely. Third, a dE is sometimes a sock in the seal. Look before you replace anything.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does 4E mean on a Samsung washing machine?

4E is a water supply error. Check the tap, the hose and the inlet filter.

What does 5E mean?

5E is a drain error. Clean the pump filter and check the hose.

Why does my Samsung keep showing UE?

The load is unbalanced. If it happens with small loads, the machine may not be level or the suspension may be worn.
