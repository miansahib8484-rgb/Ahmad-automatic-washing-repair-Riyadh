How Much Does Washing Machine Repair Cost in Riyadh?

Quick Answer

Washing machine repair cost in Riyadh depends mainly on the fault, the part, the brand and the age of the machine. A blocked filter is cheap and quick; a motor or control board costs more. Ask for the price before work starts.

By the Ahmad Automatic Washing Repair team · Updated October 2026

It is the first question everyone asks, and a fair one. The honest answer is that there is no single number, and anyone who quotes a flat price without seeing the machine is guessing.

What you can do is understand what moves the price, so you can judge a quote when you get one. We do not publish fixed prices here because they would be wrong half the time.

What Moves the Price

FactorWhy it matters
The faultA blocked filter or hose is quick. A motor, bearing set or control board takes longer and costs more.
The part and the brandPart prices differ by brand and model. Common models are easier to source.
Age of the machineOlder or rarer models can mean harder-to-find parts and longer waits.
Visit and diagnosisAsk whether the visit is charged separately, or included in the repair price.

Repair or Replace

A useful rule of thumb: if the repair costs a large share of a similar new machine, and the machine is already old, think twice. If it is under about eight years old and one part has failed, repair is usually the sensible route. Our signs your washing machine needs a repair guide helps.

Before you agree to anything, ask three things: what exactly has failed, what the total will be including the part, and what warranty comes with the work. We wrote a short checklist in how to choose a washing machine technician.
