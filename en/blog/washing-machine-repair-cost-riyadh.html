How Much Does Washing Machine Repair Cost in Riyadh?

Quick Answer

The washing machine repair price depends mainly on the fault, the part that has to be replaced and the brand. A technician can only give a firm figure after diagnosing the machine.

By the Ahmad Automatic Washing Repair team · Updated October 2026

Prices vary a lot, and anyone who quotes a fixed number over the phone without seeing the machine is guessing. This guide explains what changes the washing machine repair cost, how washing machine replacement compares, and how to compare quotes fairly.

Cheaper repairs are usually a blocked filter or hose, a worn belt, a door seal or a drain pump. More expensive repairs are a motor, a control board or drum bearings, because the part costs more and the job takes longer. Brand and model also matter, since some parts are harder to source.

Ask what the quote includes: the visit, the diagnosis, the labour, the part and any warranty on the work. Ask whether a diagnosis fee is deducted if you go ahead. A clear answer to these questions matters more than the lowest number.

If the quote is close to the price of a new machine, compare both. A repair is often worth it for a machine with a single fault, while an old machine with several faults may not be.

Questions to Ask Before You Agree

  • What is the total, including the visit and the part?
  • Is the diagnosis fee deducted if I go ahead?
  • Is the part new or used?
  • How long is the warranty on the repair?
  • Can you confirm the price on WhatsApp?

What Changes the Price

FactorWhy it changes the price
The faultA blocked filter is quick. A motor or control board takes longer and costs more.
The partPart prices differ by brand and model.
Age and brand of the machineOlder or less common models can take longer to repair or to find parts for.
Visit and diagnosisAsk whether this is charged separately or included.
Warranty on the workA repair with a stated warranty is worth more than one without.

Ask for the figure on WhatsApp or in writing before work starts.

How to Compare Quotes

  1. Give every technician the same details: brand, model and the symptom or error code
  2. Ask each one what the quote includes
  3. Compare the total and the warranty, not just the first number
  4. Choose the one that explains the fault clearly
