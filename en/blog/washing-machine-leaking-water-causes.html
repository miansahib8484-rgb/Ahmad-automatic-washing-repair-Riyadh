Washing Machine Leaking Water: Causes and Repair

Quick Answer

A washing machine leaking water usually has a worn door seal, loose or cracked hoses, a drawer overflow or a leaking pump.

By the Ahmad Automatic Washing Repair team · Updated October 2026

A leaking washer can damage your floor, so act early. If you need washing machine repair, read these causes and quick checks, then book washing machine repair and service with our team. Regular washing machine maintenance also helps prevent leaks.

Common Causes

  • Worn door seal or gasket
  • Loose or cracked inlet hoses
  • Detergent drawer overflow
  • Leaking drain pump or hose
  • Overloaded drum

What to Check First

  1. Turn off the water tap and unplug the machine
  2. Dry the floor to see where the water appears
  3. Check the hoses at the tap and at the back of the machine
  4. Leak continues? Call us to repair it

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my washing machine leaking water?

The usual causes are a worn door seal, loose hoses, drawer overflow or a leaking pump.

Can a leaking washing machine be repaired?

Usually yes. Gasket, hose and pump leaks are common and repairable.
