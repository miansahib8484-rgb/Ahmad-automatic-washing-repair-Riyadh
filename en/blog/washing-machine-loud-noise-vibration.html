Washing Machine Making Loud Noise or Shaking: Causes and Fixes

Quick Answer

A washing machine that shakes or bangs is usually unlevel, unbalanced or carrying a stray object. Grinding that comes with every spin points to worn bearings, and thumping on heavy loads can mean tired shock absorbers.

By the Ahmad Automatic Washing Repair team · Updated October 2026

A washer that sounds like it is about to walk across the room gets attention fast. The good news: many noise problems are about how the machine is sitting or what is in it, not what is broken.

The sound itself is a clue, so listen before you act.

Match the Sound to the Cause

Banging or thumping during the spin

Usually an unbalanced load or an unlevel machine. Try spreading the clothes and checking that all four feet touch the floor.

Rattling or clicking

Often a coin, a bra wire or a button in the drum or the pump. Check pockets and the pump filter.

Grinding or rumbling that rises with speed

This is the classic worn drum bearing sound. It needs a technician soon, because a worn bearing can damage the drum.

Squealing

Can be a worn belt on belt-driven machines, or a dry pump or motor.

Heavy thumping only with big loads

Tired shock absorbers or suspension springs.

Quick Fixes to Try

  1. Check that the machine is level. Rock it gently from each corner. Adjust the feet if it moves.
  2. On a new or newly moved machine, check that the transport bolts at the back were removed.
  3. Spread the load, and avoid washing one heavy item on its own.
  4. Check pockets, then clean the pump filter.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my washing machine so loud?

Common reasons are an unlevel machine, an unbalanced load, a stray object or worn bearings.

Why does my washing machine shake during the spin?

Mostly an unbalanced load or an unlevel machine. If it continues with a light load, worn shock absorbers or bearings are possible.

Do I need to remove transport bolts?

On a new machine, yes. They hold the drum steady during delivery and must come out before use.
