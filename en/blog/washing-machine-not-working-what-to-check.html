Washing Machine Not Working: What to Check First

Quick Answer

If a washing machine is completely dead, check the socket, the plug, the breaker and that the door is fully closed. If it has power but will not start, look at the child lock, delay start and the door latch.

By the Ahmad Automatic Washing Repair team · Updated October 2026

You press the button and nothing happens. No hum, no lights, or maybe lights and no movement. Before you book anything, it is worth five minutes of dull checking, because a surprising number of "dead" washers turn out to be a loose plug or a tripped breaker.

Go through these in order.

Checks in Order

  1. Try another appliance in the same socket. If it does not work either, the socket or the breaker is the problem.
  2. Check the plug and the cable for damage. If you use an extension cord, plug the washer straight into the wall instead.
  3. Look at your breaker panel for a tripped switch.
  4. Make sure the door is fully closed and the latch has clicked.
  5. Check for child lock or delay start on the display.
  6. Press and hold the power button for a few seconds, or unplug the machine for five minutes and try again. This resets some boards.

If It Has Power but Will Not Start

A display that lights up but will not begin a cycle often means the machine does not think the door is shut, or it is waiting for water. Check the tap and the door seal. A code on the display is useful here, so look at our error code finder.

A machine that dies after a power cut, or resets itself, may have a damaged board. We wrote about why in protecting your washing machine from power surges.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my washing machine completely dead?

Start with the socket, plug and breaker. If those are fine, the door lock, power cable or control board may be at fault.

The display lights up but nothing happens. Why?

Often the door is not detected as closed, child lock is on, or the machine is waiting for water. Check those first.

Can a power cut damage my washing machine?

It can damage the control board, especially if power returns with a surge. Wait a few minutes before switching it back on.
