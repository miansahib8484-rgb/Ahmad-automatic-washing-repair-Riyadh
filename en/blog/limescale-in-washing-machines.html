Limescale in Washing Machines: Signs, Causes and How to Prevent It

Quick Answer

If your water leaves white scale on taps and kettles, it is likely hard and can build up inside a washing machine. Clean the inlet filter, use a suitable descaler as the manual advises and avoid overusing detergent.

By the Ahmad Automatic Washing Repair team · Updated October 2026

Hard water leaves mineral deposits as it heats and dries. Inside a washing machine those deposits can coat the heating element and clog small filters and valves. You can check your own water first: white crusty marks on taps, shower heads and the kettle are the usual sign.

Signs of Scale Build-Up

  • White or grey crust on the door seal, the drum or the detergent drawer
  • Clothes that feel stiff or look dull after washing
  • Water that heats slowly or a heating fault
  • Slow filling because the inlet filter is partly blocked
  • Reduced water flow from small hoses and valves

How to Reduce Scale

  1. Clean the inlet filter every few months by closing the tap, unscrewing the hose and rinsing the mesh
  2. Run a maintenance wash with a descaler made for washing machines, following the product and the machine manual
  3. Never mix cleaning chemicals, and check the manual before using vinegar or other acids on rubber seals
  4. Wipe the door seal and leave the door ajar after use
  5. Use the detergent amount on the pack, because too much leaves residue that mixes with scale
  6. If your water is very hard, ask about an inline filter or water softener for the home

When Scale Becomes a Repair

Heavy scale can shorten the life of the heating element and block the inlet valve. If the machine fills slowly, does not heat or leaves a white residue, book a visit so the cause can be confirmed. Our guide on a washing machine that will not fill covers the first checks.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if I have hard water?

White crusty marks on taps, shower heads and the kettle are the usual sign. Test strips for water hardness are inexpensive and give a clearer answer.

Can limescale damage a washing machine?

Over time it can coat the heating element and block filters and valves, which can cause heating and filling faults.

How often should I descale a washing machine?

It depends on your water and how often you wash. Follow the descaler instructions and the machine manual, and clean the inlet filter every few months.
