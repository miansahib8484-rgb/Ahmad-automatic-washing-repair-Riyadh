Simple Dryer Maintenance Tips for Riyadh Homes

Quick Answer

Clean the lint filter after every load, check the vent and the area behind the dryer regularly, do not overload it, and never leave it running while you are away from home.

By the Ahmad Automatic Washing Repair team · Updated October 2026

A dryer is one of those appliances you only think about when it stops working. But a few small habits decide whether it lasts years or struggles along, and they also affect safety.

Dust is a fact of life in Riyadh, and it settles around vents and behind appliances too. So the routine matters a little more here.

Habits That Make a Difference

  1. Clean the lint filter after every load. It takes ten seconds and is the single best habit.
  2. Wipe the filter housing and the door seal now and then.
  3. Check the vent hose and the outlet. Make sure it is not crushed, kinked or packed with lint and dust.
  4. Do not overload the drum. Clothes need room to tumble for hot air to reach them.
  5. Do not dry items with oil, solvents or chemicals on them.
  6. Do not leave the dryer running when you leave the house or go to sleep.
  7. Keep the space around the dryer clear so air can circulate.

Signs It Needs Attention

Longer drying times, a hot smell, a very hot outer casing or clothes that stay damp usually point at restricted airflow. See why a dryer is not heating. If you notice any of these, stop using it and have it checked.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I clean the lint filter?

After every load.

Why is my dryer taking longer than it used to?

Usually restricted airflow from a full lint filter or a blocked vent.

Is a hot dryer casing normal?

Warm is normal. Very hot, or a burning smell, is not. Stop using it and have it checked.
