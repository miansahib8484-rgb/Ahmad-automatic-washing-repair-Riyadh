How to Choose a Washing Machine Technician in Riyadh: 7 Things to Check

Quick Answer

Choose a technician who diagnoses first, explains the fault in plain words, confirms the price before starting, tells you which parts are replaced and states any warranty clearly.

By the Ahmad Automatic Washing Repair team · Updated October 2026

Search for a washing machine technician in Riyadh and you get a wall of phone numbers that all sound the same. These seven checks work on anyone, including us, and they save you from the most common surprises.

Seven Things to Check

  1. Diagnosis before repair. A good technician finds the fault first and then explains it. Be wary of a firm price before anyone has seen the machine.
  2. A clear explanation. You should be able to say back what is wrong and what will be done. If the answer is vague, ask again.
  3. Price confirmed before work starts. Ask for the visit fee and the repair price, and keep it in a WhatsApp message or in writing.
  4. Which parts are replaced. Ask what is being changed, why, and whether you can see the old part.
  5. Warranty stated clearly. Ask how long the repair and the part are covered, and what is excluded. A number of days is better than a general promise.
  6. Specialisation. Someone who repairs washing machines regularly usually finds the fault faster than a general handyman.
  7. Payment terms. Be careful with requests to pay everything before any work is done. Paying after the machine has been tested is safer.

Before You Call

Note the brand, the model number on the door frame or back panel, the code if there is one, and what the machine does or does not do. A short video helps. The clearer your description, the more useful the first conversation will be.

If the quote is close to the price of a new machine, ask for an honest opinion on whether the repair is worth it for your model and its age. See also what affects the cost of repair.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should a technician charge before seeing the machine?

Practices differ, but you should know the visit fee and what it covers before the technician arrives.

How do I know the repair was done properly?

Ask the technician to run a full cycle before leaving and check that the original fault is gone.

Is a warranty on washing machine repairs normal?

Many services offer one, but the length and the coverage differ. Ask for the details in writing.
