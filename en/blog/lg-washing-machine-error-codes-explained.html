LG Washing Machine Error Codes: What OE, UE, IE, LE and dE Mean

Quick Answer

OE means the machine cannot drain, UE an unbalanced load, IE a water supply problem, LE a locked or overloaded motor and dE a door problem. Most can be checked in minutes.

By the Ahmad Automatic Washing Repair team · Updated October 2026

LG washers show short codes on the display when something is wrong. Here is what the most common LG washing machine error codes usually mean, what you can check safely, and when to call. Exact codes can differ by model, so check your manual if your code is not listed.

Always switch the machine off and unplug it before opening any filter or hose, and keep a towel and a shallow tray ready, because water will come out. Never force the door open while there is water in the drum.

A code that disappears after you fix the cause, such as spreading a load evenly for UE, is a good sign. A code that keeps coming back usually points to a worn part, such as the drain pump, the inlet valve, the shock absorbers or the motor, and that needs a technician.

Before You Call

  • Switch off, unplug and keep a towel ready
  • Check the drain hose and pump filter (OE)
  • Spread the load evenly (UE)
  • Open the tap fully and clean the inlet filter (IE)
  • Close the door firmly and clear the seal (dE)

LG Error Codes and First Checks

CodeWhat it usually means and what to check first
OEDrain error. Check the drain hose for kinks and clean the pump filter. If it returns, the drain pump may have failed.
UEUnbalanced load. Open the door and spread the clothes evenly. If it repeats with small loads, the suspension may be worn.
IEWater inlet error. Open the tap fully, check the supply hose and clean the inlet filter. A faulty inlet valve is the next suspect.
LEMotor locked or overloaded. Reduce the load and restart. If it returns, the motor or its sensor may need checking.
dEDoor error. Close the door firmly and clear anything caught in the seal. A faulty lock or switch may need replacing.

This is a general reference, not a diagnosis. The same code can have more than one cause.

When to Call a Technician

  1. The same code returns after your checks
  2. You hear grinding, humming or banging along with the code
  3. Water leaks or the door will not unlock
  4. You are not comfortable opening filters or hoses

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I keep using the washer with an OE code?

Not with water inside. Drain it, clear the filter and hose, and if the code returns have the pump checked.

What does the UE code mean on an LG washer?

It means the load is unbalanced. Spread the clothes evenly. Repeated UE with small loads can mean worn shock absorbers.
