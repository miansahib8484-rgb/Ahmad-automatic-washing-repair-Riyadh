Washing Machine Repair in North Riyadh

Quick Answer

We repair automatic washing machines at homes across north Riyadh, including Al Malqa, Hittin, Al Yasmin, Al Narjis and Al Arid. A technician visits, finds the fault and repairs it on site where possible.

North Riyadh is a mix of villas with proper laundry rooms and apartment buildings where the washer lives in a cupboard or on a balcony. Both are fine. What changes is how much room the technician has to pull the machine forward and reach the hoses.

Call or WhatsApp +966 55 939 1603 with your district, the brand and what the machine is doing, and we will take it from there.

Where We Go in the North

We visit Al Malqa, Al Yasmin, Al Narjis, Al Arid and Hittin, along with other north Riyadh neighbourhoods. Each of the first four has its own page with the faults people ask us about there.

If your street is not in that list, ask. We will tell you honestly if it is a stretch.

A Small Favour That Saves Time

Please clear a little space around the machine and keep the water tap within reach. If it is a built-in unit, tell us. It lets the technician pull the machine forward, look at the hoses and test the water supply without moving half the kitchen.

Before the visit, note the brand, the model number on the door frame or back panel and any code on the display. A short phone video of the fault, such as the drum refusing to spin, helps us come prepared.
