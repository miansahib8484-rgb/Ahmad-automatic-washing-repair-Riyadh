Our technician repairs Samsung front load and top load washers at your home in Riyadh. Water supply, drain, door and balance codes are the most common starting points.

Samsung washers are quick to tell you something is wrong. A short code appears, the cycle stops, and you are left with a drum full of wet clothes. The code is useful, though. It narrows things down to water in, water out, the door or the load.

Water In, Water Out

Supply and drain codes make up the bulk of the Samsung problems people ask us about. Before you call, check that the tap is fully open and that the drain hose is not folded behind the machine. If the code stays after that, the inlet filter or the drain pump may be blocked, and that is usually a quick job to confirm.

A 5E that returns every few cycles is worth taking seriously. A pump that is struggling can fail completely, and a failed pump on a front loader often means a flooded floor. We explain how to check the filter yourself in our guide to a washing machine that will not drain.

Door and Balance Faults

A dE or dC code means the machine does not see the door as shut. Sometimes it is a bit of clothing caught in the seal. Sometimes the lock has worn out. For UE, spreading the wet clothes usually solves it, but repeated UE with small loads can mean the machine is not level or the suspension is tired. Our Samsung error codes guide goes through each one.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you repair Samsung washing machines at home?

Yes. We repair Samsung front load and top load washers at your home across Riyadh.

What does 5E mean on a Samsung washing machine?

5E is a drain error. Check the hose and clean the pump filter. If it comes back, the pump may be failing.

Is a Samsung dE code always a broken door lock?

No. It is often something caught in the seal or a door that has not clicked shut. If it persists, the lock may need replacing.
