Quick Answer

Our Samsung washing machine technician repairs front load and top load Samsung washers at your home in Riyadh, including spin, drain, leak and error-code faults.

Need a Samsung washing machine technician in Riyadh? We offer Samsung washing machine service at your door: we diagnose the fault, explain the repair and fix it on site where possible. Call +966 55 939 1603 or WhatsApp us to book a visit.

Samsung washers show short codes on the display when something is wrong. Tell us the code when you call and we can usually say what it points to before the visit, though one code can have several causes.

Water supply and drain codes are common. Check that the tap is fully open and the drain hose is not kinked. If the code stays, the inlet filter or the drain pump may be blocked.

We are an independent repair service and are not affiliated with Samsung.

Common Problems

  • Samsung washing machine not spinning
  • Samsung front load washer leaking

  • Noise or shaking during the spin cycle

Common Samsung Error Codes

CodeWhat it usually means
4E / 4CWater supply error: often a closed tap or a blocked inlet filter.
5E / 5CDrain error: usually a blocked pump or hose.
dE / dCDoor error: the door is not detected as fully closed or locked.

This is a general reference, not a diagnosis. The same code can have more than one cause.
