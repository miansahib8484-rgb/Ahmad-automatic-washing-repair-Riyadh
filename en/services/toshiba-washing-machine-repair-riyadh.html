Toshiba Washing Machine Repair in Riyadh

Quick Answer

We repair Toshiba automatic washing machines at your home in Riyadh. Drum, drain, leak and door faults are what most visits are about.

Toshiba washers are simple, sturdy machines, and that works in your favour when something goes wrong. The faults tend to be few and clear: the drum will not spin, the water will not drain, or there is a leak around the door.

Send us the model number and a short description on WhatsApp (+966 55 939 1603), or call. A twenty-second video of the drum failing to turn tells us more than five minutes of explanation.

Three Checks That Cover Most Toshiba Faults

First the drain pump and its filter, because a blocked pump is behind a large share of "will not drain" and even some "will not spin" calls. Then the belt, if your model has one, since a stretched or slipped belt shows up as weak spinning. Then the door seal, which is where leaks tend to start.

If those three are fine, we move on to the motor, the lock and the control board. We explain each step, so you know why a part is being replaced and not just that it is. Related reading: why a washing machine will not spin and where leaks come from.

Typical Toshiba Complaints

None of these automatically means the machine is finished.

  • Drum not spinning
  • Water not draining
  • Water leaking around the door or underneath
  • The cycle will not start
  • Noise or shaking during the spin
