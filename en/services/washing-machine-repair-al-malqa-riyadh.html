Washing Machine Repair in Al Malqa, Riyadh

Quick Answer

We repair automatic washing machines at homes in Al Malqa, north Riyadh. Banging, shaking and drums that will not spin are the usual reasons for a call.

A washing machine that sounds like it is trying to leave the room is a common reason for a call from Al Malqa. We visit Al Malqa and the surrounding north Riyadh neighbourhoods, find out what is behind the noise or the stalled drum and repair it on site where possible. Call or WhatsApp +966 55 939 1603 with your address.

Before you ring, check three things. Is the machine level? Is the load balanced? Is there one heavy item, a blanket or a towel, wound around the rest? Those cause a lot of the shaking. If the noise continues with a light, even load, read our guide on loud noise and vibration, or book a visit.

If your machine is on an upper floor or inside a building with gate access, mention it when you book. A short video of the noise, or of the drum not turning, helps a great deal.
