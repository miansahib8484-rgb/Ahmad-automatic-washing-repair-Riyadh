Front Load Washing Machine Repair in Riyadh

Quick Answer

We repair front load and top load automatic washers at your home in Riyadh. On front loaders the usual trouble spots are the door lock, the door seal, the drain pump and the bearings.

Front load washers wash well and fail in predictable places. That is a strange thing to say as a selling point, but it matters: predictable faults are quick to check. If the door will not open, the machine leaks near the glass, the drum makes a grinding noise or the laundry room smells like a pond, we already have a short list of suspects.

The Usual Suspects on a Front Loader

The door lock

It keeps the door shut during a cycle and releases it afterwards. When it wears, the machine either will not start or will not let you in. Do not force the handle. Our guide on releasing a stuck door shows the safe way.

The door seal

This rubber ring collects lint, hair and damp. It splits over time, and when it does, water escapes at the door. A musty smell often starts here too. A regular wipe-down helps, as in our monthly cleaning routine.

The drain pump

A coin or a sock is enough to jam it. If water stays in the drum, this is the first place to look. See what to check when it will not drain.

The drum bearings

Grinding or rumbling that rises as the drum speeds up is the classic bearing noise. It deserves a prompt visit, because a worn bearing can damage the drum.

And Top Loaders

We repair top load automatic machines too. They have their own habits: belts, lid switches and agitators instead of door seals and bearings at the front. Whichever you own, tell us the brand and the symptom, and we will plan from there. Brand pages: LG and Samsung.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you repair front load washing machines at home?

Yes. We repair front load and top load automatic washers at your home across Riyadh.

Why does my front load washer smell musty?

Usually damp trapped in the door seal and detergent drawer. Wipe the seal, leave the door ajar between washes and run a cleaning cycle monthly.

Is a grinding noise a bearing problem?

It can be. Noise that rises with drum speed, even with a light load, often points to the bearings. A technician can confirm.
