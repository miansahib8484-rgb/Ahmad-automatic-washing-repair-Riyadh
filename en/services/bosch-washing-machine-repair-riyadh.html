Bosch Washing Machine Repair in Riyadh

Quick Answer

We repair Bosch front load and top load washing machines at your home in Riyadh: drain, fill, spin, leak and error-code faults.

Bosch washers have a reputation for running quietly, which makes it all the more annoying when one stops with a short code and a drum full of water. Bosch machines tell you something is wrong with a letter and a number, usually E or F followed by two digits.

Call or WhatsApp +966 55 939 1603 with the code and the model number. We can often say what it points to before the visit, though one code can have more than one cause.

Common Bosch Codes

CodeWhat it usually meansFirst thing to check
E17 / F17Water is not filling, or filling too slowlyTap open, inlet hose not kinked, inlet filter clean
E18 / F18Water is not draining in timeDrain filter, hose and pump
F16Door not detected as closedClose firmly, check the seal
F21Drum drive problemRemove part of the load; if it repeats, book a visit
F23Leak protection triggered, on models that have itUnplug and look for water in the base

Bosch uses E or F codes depending on the model series, and codes can have more than one cause. This is a general reference, not a diagnosis.

Fill and Drain First

Fill and drain codes are by far the ones people ask about. Check that the tap is fully open, the inlet filter is clean and the drain hose is not kinked. If the code comes back, the inlet valve or the drain pump may need attention. Our guide to Bosch error codes goes through each code, and you can also try the error code finder.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you repair Bosch washing machines at home?

Yes. We repair Bosch front load and top load automatic washers at your home across Riyadh.

What do Bosch error codes E17 and E18 mean?

E17 usually means the machine is not getting enough water, and E18 means it cannot drain. We find the cause and repair it.

What should I have ready when I call about my Bosch washer?

The model number on the door frame or back panel, the code on the display and a short description of the fault.
