Midea Washing Machine Repair in Riyadh

Quick Answer

Our technician repairs Midea front load and top load washing machines at your home in Riyadh, including drain, fill, spin, leak and error-code faults.

Looking for Midea washing machine repair in Riyadh? Our technician visits your home, diagnoses the fault, explains the repair and fixes it on site where possible. Call +966 55 939 1603 or WhatsApp us to book.

Midea error codes differ a lot between models, so the same code can mean different things on two machines. Send us the model number from the door frame or back panel, the code on the display and a short video of the problem, and we can usually prepare before the visit.

Many faults have a simple first check. A washer that will not fill often has a closed tap or a blocked inlet filter, and one that will not drain often has a clogged drain filter.

We are an independent repair service and are not affiliated with Midea.

Common Problems

  • Midea washing machine not draining
  • Midea washer not filling with water
  • Drum not spinning
  • Water leaking from the door or the base
  • Door will not open at the end of the cycle
  • Noise or shaking during the spin cycle

What to Check First

FaultFirst check
Not filling with waterTap fully open, inlet hose not kinked, inlet filter clean.
Not drainingDrain filter clear of lint and coins, drain hose not kinked.
Not spinningLoad not too heavy or unbalanced, door fully closed, drain not blocked.
LeakingDoor seal and drain filter cap seated properly, hoses tight.
Will not startSocket working, door fully closed, start pressed after the program is selected.

These are general first checks, not a diagnosis. Unplug the machine before checking filters or hoses. If the fault stays, book a visit.
