LG Washing Machine Repair in Riyadh

Quick Answer

We repair LG front load and top load washing machines at your home in Riyadh. Drain, balance, door and motor faults are the usual suspects, and the code on the display tells us where to start.

LG washers are in a lot of Riyadh homes, so the same handful of faults keep turning up. The helpful part is that LG codes are fairly specific. OE, UE, IE, LE and dE each point at a different part of the machine, and knowing which one you have before we arrive helps us bring the right tools.

What the Common LG Codes Usually Mean

CodeWhat it usually meansWorth checking first
OEWater is not drainingKinked hose, then the pump filter

dEDoor not detected as closedClose firmly, clear the seal

One code can have more than one cause, and codes vary by model. Treat this as a starting point.

How an LG Visit Usually Goes

On many LG front loaders the pump filter sits behind a small panel at the bottom front, which makes a drain fault quick to check. For a machine that will not spin we look at the load, the door lock and the motor. Many LG front loaders use a direct drive motor with no belt, so a no-spin fault is approached differently from an older belt-driven machine.

Door faults deserve a careful look. A dE code can be a seal with something caught in it, or a lock that has worn out. We check the simple one first, and we explain what we find before replacing anything. More on codes in our LG error codes guide and the error code finder.

We are an independent repair service and are not affiliated with LG.

Before You Call

Unplug the machine and clear the pump filter if you can reach it, spread the load if it is a UE, and make sure the tap is fully open for an IE. If the code returns, tell us what you tried. It saves time on the visit.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you repair LG washing machines at home?

Yes. We repair LG front load and top load washers at your home across Riyadh.

What does OE mean on an LG washer?

OE is a drain error. Check the hose for kinks and clean the pump filter. If it returns, the pump may need replacing.

My LG keeps showing UE even with a small load. Why?

Repeated UE with small loads can point to worn suspension or a machine that is not level. A technician can check which.
