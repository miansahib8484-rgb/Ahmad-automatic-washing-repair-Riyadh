Quick Answer

The number is +966 55 939 1603.

What We Check First

We repair front load and top load automatic machines. On most visits we check the door lock, the pump, the belt and the seal first, because these cause most no-spin, no-drain and leak faults. Then the motor, the sensors and the control board if the simple parts are fine.

We explain what we find at each step, and tell you the cost before any repair starts. If a part has to be ordered, we say so and give you an honest idea of how long it takes.
