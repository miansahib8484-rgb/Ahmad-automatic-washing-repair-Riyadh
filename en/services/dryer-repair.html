We only repair automatic washing machines, dishwashers and dryers. No ACs, fridges, ovens or TVs. Staying with three appliances means we keep meeting the same faults, and that is what makes them quick to recognise.

You open the dryer after a full cycle and the clothes are still damp, and warm only in a hopeful sort of way. Or the drum has stopped turning and the machine hums to itself. Those two stories cover most of the dryer calls we hear.

Both are usually straightforward to diagnose at home. Airflow, the heating parts and the drum drive are checked in a sensible order, and we explain what we find.

What Usually Goes Wrong

No heat, or not enough

The usual suspects are the heating element, a thermostat or thermal fuse, and, very often, a blocked vent. A dryer that cannot push hot air out cannot dry. See why a dryer is not heating.

Clothes still wet after a full cycle

Check the lint filter and the vent first. If the airflow is good, the heater or a sensor may be at fault.

The drum will not turn

A snapped or stretched belt is the classic cause. A worn motor or roller can do it too.

Noise

Squealing or thumping often comes from worn rollers or the belt.

Will not start

Door switch, thermal fuse, power supply or control board.

Overheating

Almost always restricted airflow. Please stop using the dryer and have it checked.

Why Lint Matters More Than You Think

Lint is not only the reason for a slow dryer. A build-up in the filter, the vent or the duct is a fire risk and makes the heater work harder. Clean the lint filter after every load, and have the vent checked from time to time. Our dryer maintenance tips for Riyadh homes cover the routine.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my dryer not heating?

Common causes are a blocked vent, a faulty heating element or a failed thermostat. A technician can check each one.

Why are my clothes still wet after a full cycle?

Usually restricted airflow from a full lint filter or a blocked vent. If the airflow is fine, it may be a heating fault.

Can you repair a dryer that will not spin?

Often yes. A worn belt, roller or motor is repairable.

Is it safe to keep using an overheating dryer?

No. Stop using it and have it checked. Overheating usually means restricted airflow.

Do you offer same-day service?

We try to where the schedule allows. Morning calls have the best chance.
