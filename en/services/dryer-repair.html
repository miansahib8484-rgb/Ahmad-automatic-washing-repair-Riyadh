What Causes These Faults?

Why is my dryer not heating?

Usually a burnt-out heating element, a tripped thermal fuse, or a faulty thermostat.

Why are my clothes still wet after a full cycle?

Often a blocked vent restricting airflow, a failing heating element, or an overloaded drum.

Why is my dryer drum not spinning?

Commonly a broken or slipped drive belt, a worn drum roller, or a faulty motor.

Why is my dryer making loud noise?

Usually worn drum rollers or bearings, a damaged belt, or an object caught between the drum and the housing.
