A washing machine rarely dies all at once. Usually one part gives up while everything else keeps working: a pump clogs, a belt slips, a door lock stops catching. That is good news, because a single failed part is usually a repair and not a reason to buy a new machine.

What Usually Goes Wrong

Water stays in the drum

Very often it is the drain. A sock, a coin or a ball of lint sits in the pump filter, or the hose is kinked behind the machine. If the filter is easy to reach, unplug the machine, put a towel and a tray under it and clear it. If water is still there afterwards, the pump itself may have failed. We wrote a step-by-step guide on a washing machine that will not drain.

The drum will not spin

A drum that turns by hand but will not spin during the cycle can mean a worn belt on older machines, worn carbon brushes, or a motor or control fault. Slow spinning with a humming sound is a clue worth mentioning when you call. Before you do, open the door and spread the wet clothes out, because an unbalanced load stops the spin on most machines. More in why a washing machine will not spin.

Water on the floor

Front loaders leak at the door seal, at the drain filter cap or at a loose hose far more often than from the drum itself. So a puddle does not always mean a big repair. Look for where the water is coming from before you decide. See where washing machine leaks come from.

Banging, grinding or shaking

A bunched-up load and an unlevel machine cause most of the shaking. Grinding that comes with every spin, even with a light load, is more often worn drum bearings. Our guide on loud noise and vibration helps you tell the two apart.

The door will not open

Washers keep the door locked while there is water inside, and while the lock is resetting after a cycle. If it stays shut for good, do not pull the handle. Read how to release a stuck door safely.

Dead, or restarting by itself

Check the socket and that the door is fully closed. A machine that resets on its own or dies after a power cut may have a damaged control board. Our note on protecting the board from power surges explains why.

Error Codes We Are Asked About Most

Codes look scary, but most of them describe the symptom rather than the broken part. Our error code finder gives you the first things to check, and the guides for LG, Samsung and Bosch go further.

Brand and codeWhat it usually points to
LG OEDrain error. The water is not leaving the drum fast enough.
LG UEUnbalanced load. Redistribute the clothes; repeated UE with small loads can mean worn suspension.
LG IEWater inlet error. Tap, hose and inlet filter first, then the inlet valve.
LG LEMotor locked or overloaded. Reduce the load; if it returns, the motor or its sensor needs checking.
LG dEDoor error. The machine does not see the door as closed.
Samsung 4E / 4CWater supply error. Often a closed tap or a blocked inlet filter.
Samsung 5E / 5CDrain error, usually a blocked pump filter or hose.
Samsung dE / dCDoor error. Close firmly and clear the seal.
Bosch E17 / E18E17 is a fill problem and E18 a drain problem, though newer series show F17 and F18.

A code is a hint, not a verdict. The same code can have more than one cause, and codes differ between model series.

Repair or Replace?

Most faults are worth repairing, especially on a machine under eight years old with a single failed part. Pumps, belts, door seals, sensors and control boards are all repairable items.

It gets harder when the machine is old, when repairs keep arriving for unrelated faults, or when the quote comes close to the price of a similar new machine. A cracked outer drum or a major electrical burn-out usually points to replacement. Our guide on signs your washing machine needs a repair and the one on what repairs cost in Riyadh will help you weigh it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you fix a washing machine that will not drain?

Yes, drain faults are among the most common ones we repair. Usually it is a blocked filter or hose, and sometimes a failed pump.

Do you repair different washing machine brands?

Yes. We repair LG, Samsung, Daewoo, Toshiba, Bosch, Midea, Hitachi, Haier, Whirlpool and others, in front load and top load. Tell us the brand and model when you call.

Do you repair at my home in Riyadh?

Yes. A technician visits your home anywhere in Riyadh and repairs the machine on site where possible.

How much does washing machine repair cost in Riyadh?

It depends on the fault and the part. A blocked filter is quick and a control board is not. Call us with the brand, model and symptom and we will explain what to expect. You will know the cost before the work starts.

Do you offer same-day repair?

We try to, where the schedule and your location allow. Morning calls have the best chance.

Is there a warranty on repairs?

Yes, completed repairs come with a workmanship warranty. Ask the technician what it covers and for how long.

How long does a repair take?

A blocked filter or hose can be cleared in one visit. If a part has to be ordered, it takes longer, and we tell you that when we diagnose the fault.

What does OE mean on an LG washing machine?

OE is a drain error. Check the drain hose for kinks and clean the pump filter. If it comes back, the drain pump may need replacing.

Can I repair a washing machine myself?

You can check the filter, the hoses, the tap and the load balance. Pumps, motors, belts and boards are better left to a technician, and always unplug the machine first.
