Common Washing Machine Error Codes

These are the error codes we see most often on LG and Samsung washing machines in Riyadh homes. The exact letter can vary slightly by model, but the fault they point to is usually one of these.

CodeWhat it usually means
LG: OE / 5EDrain error — water isn't draining out of the machine.
LG: UEUnbalanced load — usually resolves itself, but can point to a suspension issue if it repeats.
LG: LEMotor locked or overloaded — often a drum or motor fault.
LG: PEWater level sensor error.
Samsung: 4E / 4CWater supply error — often a closed tap or blocked inlet filter.
Samsung: 5E / 5CDrain error — similar to LG's OE, usually a blocked pump or hose.
Samsung: dE / dCDoor error — the door isn't detected as fully closed or locked.

This is a general reference, not a diagnosis — the same code can have more than one cause. If your machine is showing one of these, tell us the code and the brand when you call and it helps us come prepared.

What Causes These Faults?

Why is my washing machine not draining?

Usually a blocked drain pump filter, a kinked or clogged drain hose, or a faulty pressure/water-level sensor. Coins, buttons and lint are the most common blockages we find.

Why is my washing machine not spinning?

Often a worn drive belt, a failing motor or carbon brushes, or the door lock sensor not confirming the door is properly closed.

Why is my washing machine leaking water?

Most leaks trace back to a worn door seal (rubber gasket), a loose or cracked hose connection, or a damaged detergent drawer seal.

Why is my washing machine vibrating or making loud noise?

Commonly worn drum bearings, an unbalanced load, or transit bolts that were never removed after delivery or a house move.
