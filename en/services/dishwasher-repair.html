What Causes These Faults?

Why is my dishwasher not cleaning dishes properly?

Usually a clogged spray arm, a dirty filter, low water pressure, or the wrong amount or type of detergent.

Why is my dishwasher not draining?

Often a blocked drain pump or hose, a clogged filter basket, or a faulty drain valve.

Why is my dishwasher leaking water?

Commonly a worn door seal, a cracked spray arm, excess suds from too much detergent, or a loose hose connection.

Why is my dishwasher making unusual noise?

Usually a worn wash pump motor, a spray arm hitting dishes or a rack, or debris caught in the pump.
