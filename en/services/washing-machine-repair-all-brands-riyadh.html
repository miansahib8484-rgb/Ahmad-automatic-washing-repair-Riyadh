Washing Machine Repair for All Brands in Riyadh

Quick Answer

We repair washing machines of most brands sold in Riyadh, from LG and Samsung to Bosch, Midea, Hitachi, Haier, Whirlpool and others, at your home.

Walk through any Riyadh neighbourhood and you will find a mix of washers behind the doors: Korean, Japanese, German, Turkish, Chinese. Moving flats, buying second-hand and gifts from relatives all add to the mix. So the question we hear most is not about a fault. It is "do you work on mine?"

In most cases the answer is yes. Send the brand and model number on WhatsApp (+966 55 939 1603), or call, and we will tell you what to expect.

Who We Work On

Brands with their own page: LG, Samsung, Daewoo, Toshiba, Bosch and Midea. We also repair Hitachi, Haier, Whirlpool, Maytag, Siemens, Ariston, Beko, Panasonic, Westinghouse and other makes, fully automatic or semi-automatic, front load or top load.

Newer or rarer models can mean longer waits for parts. We will say so when we diagnose the fault, so you can decide with the facts in front of you.

Finding Your Model Number

Look on the door frame, the back panel or the rating sticker inside the door. A photo of that sticker, plus a photo or video of the problem, is the best way to start the conversation. If you cannot find it, tell us the brand and we will work from there.

We are an independent repair service and are not affiliated with any of these brands.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you repair every washing machine brand?

We repair most brands sold in Riyadh. If yours is unusual, send the brand and model number and we will tell you.

Do I need the model number?

It helps a lot, but it is not essential. The brand and a description of the fault are enough to start.

Are you affiliated with the brands you repair?

No. We are an independent repair service.
