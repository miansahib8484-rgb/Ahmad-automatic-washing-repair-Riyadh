Simple appliance care tips and updates from our repair team in Riyadh.

Most of what's here started as an actual customer question. If you're trying to figure out whether your washing machine needs a technician or just a quick fix you can do yourself, start with the posts below — we'll add more as new questions come up.

Washing Machines•2026

5 Signs Your Washing Machine Needs a Repair

Watch for a drum that won't spin, water pooling underneath, violent shaking during the spin cycle, clothes coming out still soaked, or a burning smell from the motor. Catching any of these early usually means a cheaper, simpler fix than waiting until the machine stops working altogether.

Dishwashers•2026

Why Is My Dishwasher Not Draining Properly?

Nine times out of ten it's a clogged filter, a blocked drain hose, or a spray arm jammed with food debris — all quick fixes. A worn-out drain pump is the less common but pricier cause, so it's worth ruling out the simple stuff first before assuming the worst.

Dryers•2026

Simple Dryer Maintenance Tips for Riyadh Homes

Clean the lint filter after every load, check the vent for blockages every few months, and avoid overloading the drum. Riyadh's dust means vents tend to clog faster here than in most places, so it's worth checking yours more often than the manual suggests.
