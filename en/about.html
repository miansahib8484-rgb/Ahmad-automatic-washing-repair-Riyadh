About the Business

About Ahmad Automatic Washing Repair

Ahmad Automatic Washing Repair started with a simple focus: fix washing machines, dishwashers and dryers properly, and only those three things. We're based in Al Quds, and most of the households we work with are a short drive from home.

We're not trying to be an everything-repair shop. Sticking to three appliance types means our technicians see the same faults often enough to recognize them fast — and fix them right the first time.

Our Mission

Our Mission

Our goal isn't complicated: get your appliance working again, tell you honestly what it needed, and not waste your time or money doing it.

Why Customers Choose Us

Most of our customers find us through a neighbor or a WhatsApp group recommendation rather than an ad — which says something about how the actual visits go.

  • Experienced, trained technicians
  • Clear explanation before any repair
  • On-site service across Riyadh
  • Focus on lasting repairs, not quick fixes

Our Repair Approach

We've seen cases where a customer was quoted for a new motor when the real problem was a blocked filter. We check the obvious things first, every time.

Riyadh Service Coverage

Al Quds is home base, but we regularly cover Al Naseem, Al Rawdah, Al Malaz, Al Olaya, Al Yasmin, Al Sulimaniyah and Al Nakheel too. If you're further out, just ask — we'll tell you honestly if it's a stretch.
