Washing Machine Error Code Finder

Quick Answer

Choose your brand and type the code shown on the display to see what it usually means and what to check first. Codes can vary by model, so check your manual too.

Find Your Code

All Codes in This Finder

BrandCodeWhat it usually means and what to check first
LGOEDrain error. Check the drain hose for kinks and clean the pump filter. If it returns, the drain pump may have failed.
LGUEUnbalanced load. Open the door and spread the clothes evenly. If it repeats with small loads, the suspension may be worn.
LGIEWater inlet error. Open the tap fully, check the supply hose and clean the inlet filter. A faulty inlet valve is the next suspect.
LGLEMotor locked or overloaded. Reduce the load and restart. If it returns, the motor or its sensor may need checking.
LGdEDoor error. Close the door firmly and clear anything caught in the seal. A faulty lock or switch may need replacing.
Samsung4E / 4CWater supply error. Open the tap fully, check the hose for kinks and clean the inlet filter.
Samsung5E / 5CDrain error. Check the drain hose and clean the pump filter. A failed drain pump is the next suspect.
SamsungdE / dCDoor error. Close the door firmly and clear the seal. A faulty door lock may need replacing.
SamsungUE / UCUnbalanced load. Spread the clothes evenly and restart. Repeated UE with small loads can mean worn suspension.

This is a general reference, not a diagnosis. The same code can have more than one cause.
