FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you repair automatic washing machines?

Yes — draining, spinning, leaking, door problems, error codes, the usual list.

Do you repair dishwashers?

Yes, including the annoying ones — dishes coming out still dirty, or water pooling at the bottom.

Do you repair dryers?

Yes — not heating, drum not turning, clothes still wet after a full cycle, all of it.

Do you provide on-site repair in Riyadh?

Yes, our technicians visit your location anywhere in Riyadh.

How can I book a repair?

Call, WhatsApp, or fill in the form — whatever's easiest for you. We usually reply within the hour.

How quickly can a technician visit?

Morning calls usually mean a same-day visit. Afternoon calls sometimes push to the next morning — we'll tell you straight away which one applies.

Do you repair different appliance brands?

Yes. We repair LG, Samsung, Daewoo, Bosch, Toshiba, Hitachi, Midea, Haier, Whirlpool, Maytag and Ariston, among others. Tell us your model when you call or WhatsApp and we will confirm.

How much does washing machine repair cost in Riyadh?

Cost depends on the fault and any parts needed. Contact us with your appliance details for a free estimate.

Do you offer same-day repair service?

We try to offer same-day visits where possible, depending on your location and our schedule.

Is there a warranty on repairs?

Yes, completed repairs come with a workmanship warranty. Ask our technician for details specific to your repair.

What does Ahmad Automatic Washing Repair repair?

We repair automatic washing machines, dishwashers and dryers at customers' homes in Riyadh.

Which areas of Riyadh do you serve?

We serve Riyadh, including north, east, west and south Riyadh. Contact us to confirm coverage for your district.

Do you repair fridges, air conditioners or ovens?

No. We focus on washing machines, dishwashers and dryers.
