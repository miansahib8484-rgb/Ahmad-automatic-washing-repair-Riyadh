Common Problems We Fix

Most calls start with a clear symptom: a door that will not release, water leaking from underneath, a drum that has stopped turning, a loud noise or shaking during the spin, or a machine that will not start. We repair front load and top load, fully automatic and semi-automatic washing machines, so whatever is in your laundry room, we have probably fixed one like it.

Dishwashers have their own usual suspects: a spray arm that has stopped turning, a dead pump, a faulty sensor or a door seal that lets water escape. Dryers are usually a heating element, a thermostat, a blocked vent or a motor on its way out.

Seeing a code such as OE or UE on an LG, 4E or 5E on a Samsung, or E17 or E18 on a Bosch? Mention it when you call and we can usually tell you what it points to before the visit. Try our error code finder or read the Bosch error codes guide. We also help track down spare parts, offer a free diagnosis and can explain what our repair warranty covers.
